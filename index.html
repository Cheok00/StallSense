How long is the wait?

Where should I eat?

How the wait is worked out

Wait = (orders waiting + your order) ÷ orders the stall finished per minute in the last 10 minutes.

  • It's shown as a range, because it's an estimate.
  • Live: measured from the last 10 minutes. Estimate: not enough recent orders yet, so it uses the stall's typical speed.
  • Recent report: the stall hasn't updated for over 2 minutes, for example because its connection dropped.
  • Estimate unavailable: no update for over 15 minutes, so no number is shown rather than a wrong one.
